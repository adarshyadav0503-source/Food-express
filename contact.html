Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

🛒 How do I place an order?+

Simply browse our Menu page, add items to your cart, go to the Order page, fill in your delivery details, choose your payment method (UPI or COD), and place your order. It's that easy!

🚚 What are the delivery charges?+

Delivery is ₹40 for orders below ₹499. Orders above ₹499 get FREE delivery! 🎉

💳 Do you accept online payments?+

Yes! We accept UPI payments via QR code (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.) and also Cash on Delivery (COD). Simply scan the QR code on the order page.

👥 Can I book a table for a large group?+

Absolutely! You can book a table for up to 10+ guests through our Book Table page. For groups larger than 15, please call us directly at +91 7678268783.

🎉 Do you cater for events?+

Yes, we provide catering services for events, parties, weddings, and corporate functions. Contact us at adarsh@foodieexpress.com for custom menus and pricing.

📦 How can I track my order?+

After placing your order, you'll receive an Order ID. You can call us at +91 7678268783 with your Order ID to get real-time updates on your delivery.
