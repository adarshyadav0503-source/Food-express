Pro Cooking Tips 💡

Master these basics and elevate every dish

🧂

Season As You Go

Add salt and spices at every stage of cooking, not just at the end. Layers of seasoning create depth of flavor.

🔥

Hot Pan, Cold Oil

Let the pan get hot before adding oil. This prevents food from sticking and gives better sear marks.

🧅

Caramelize Slowly

Cook onions on low-medium heat for 15-20 min for deep caramelization. Patience creates the best base for gravies.

🌿

Fresh Herbs Last

Add fresh herbs like coriander, mint, and basil at the very end of cooking to preserve their aroma and color.

🍋

Acid Brightens Everything

A squeeze of lemon or dash of vinegar at the end brightens any dish. It's the secret restaurant trick!

⏰

Rest Your Meat

After cooking, let meat rest for 5-10 min before cutting. This lets juices redistribute for a tender result.
