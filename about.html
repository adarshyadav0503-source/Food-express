Our Story 📖

FoodieExpress was born in 2020 from a simple idea — everyone deserves restaurant-quality food delivered to their doorstep. Founded by Adarsh Yadav in New Delhi, we started as a small kitchen with big dreams.

What began with just 10 dishes and a passion for cooking has grown into a full-fledged food platform serving 110+ dishes across Indian, Chinese, Italian, and street food cuisines. We serve 1000+ happy customers daily.

Our secret? Fresh ingredients sourced daily, recipes perfected over years, and a team of dedicated chefs who pour love into every dish. We also believe in sharing knowledge — that's why we have 20+ detailed recipes on our website so you can cook at home too!
